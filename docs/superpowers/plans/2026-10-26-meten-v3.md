# Meten v3 — implementation plan

Spec: §4.1, §4.2, §5.10, §5.14, §5.45–§5.52 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-rekenhulp-design.md`.

TDD, one commit per task: tests first, then the code, `npm test` + `npm run check`, commit.
No new answer kind, no new tip text: multiple choice uses `choiceStep`, number answers
`numberStep` (factor-of-ten fallback included). Every new topic registers in the same task:
widen `Topic` (`lib/types.ts`), add the generator to `GENERATORS` and the label to
`TOPIC_LABELS` (`lib/topics/index.ts`). The slot sweep (`slot.sweep.test.ts`) then covers it
automatically. The sets are changed only in tasks 7 and 12, so the app stays consistent between
tasks.

Shared conventions for the new topics:

- Times of day are minutes since midnight (`0`–`1439`) in code, shown with `formatClock`
  (`H:MM`, task 5).
- `'s avonds` etc. use the straight apostrophe, as in the spec.
- The number answers of the generators are `Rational` (`lib/rational.ts`); no floats.
- Keys: `<topic>:<form>:<variant or context>:<numbers>`, without the slot marker.

### Task 5: `clock` (§5.47)

Files: new `lib/topics/clock.ts` + test; `types.ts`, `index.ts` (label
`Klok (kijken en tijdsduur)`).

Helpers, exported for `timeCalc` (task 10):

- `formatClock(minutes: number): string`: `H:MM`, minutes modulo 1440.
- `spokenTime(minutes: number): string`: the phrase of the spec, minutes a multiple of 5
  (throws otherwise).
- `partOfDay(minutes: number): string`: `'s nachts` / `'s ochtends` / `'s middags` / `'s avonds`.
- `elapsedExplanation(start: number, end: number): string`: segments start → next full hour,
  the whole hours as one segment, last full hour → end; a zero-length segment is left out; with
  more than one segment it ends with `→ a + b + c = total`.

Generator, three forms equally likely:

- times for the choice forms: a multiple of 5 over the whole day, without
  `isExcluded(t) = t % 60 >= 20 && [5, 11, 17, 23].includes(Math.floor(t / 60))`
- words → digital: prompt `Hoe laat is ${spokenTime(t)} ${partOfDay(t)}?`; options
  `t`, `t + 720`, `t + 60`, `mirror(t) ?? t − 60` (all modulo 1440), sorted by minute of the
  day, shown with `formatClock`. `mirror`: minutes 20–40 → `60 − m` in the same hour; 5–15 →
  `t − 2m`; 45–55 → `t + 2(60 − m)`; 0 and 30 → none.
- digital → words: prompt `Hoe zeg je ${formatClock(t)}?`; options (shuffled)
  `spoken(t) part(t)`, `spoken(t − 60) part(t)`, `spoken(mirror(t) ?? t + 60) part(t)`,
  `spoken(t) part(t + 720)`.
- elapsed: start a multiple of 5 in `[360, 1320]`, duration a multiple of 5 in `[10, 95]`,
  `start % 60 + duration >= 60`, end `< 1440`; prompt `Van ${start} tot ${end}. Hoeveel minuten
  is dat?`, suffix `min`, explanation `elapsedExplanation`.
- explanations: words → digital `${spoken} = ${12-hour time}`, plus `→ ${part} is dat ${t}` when
  the 24-hour time differs, and `${spoken} 's nachts = 0:05` for hour 0; digital → words
  `19:35 = 7:35 → vijf over half 8 's avonds`, or `8:20 → tien voor half 9 's ochtends` when
  both notations are equal.
- keys `clock:toDigital:<t>`, `clock:toWords:<t>`, `clock:elapsed:<start>:<end>`.

Data, as the spec tables:

  je ongeveer 17 km/u.`, `Nederland heeft ongeveer 18 miljoen inwoners.`
- `INSTRUMENTS` (7 names) and `INSTRUMENT_ITEMS: { what; answer; excluded? }[]` (8); prompt
  `Waarmee meet je ${what}?`, explanation `${capitalize(what)} meet je met een ${answer}.`
- `UNIT_ITEMS: { before; after; value; options; answer; also? }[]` (14), prompt
  `${before} ${value} ${SLOT}${after}` (`In een drinkglas past ongeveer 200 ?.`), explanation
  the sentence with the answer and `also` in brackets: drinkglas `(2 dl)`, zand `(10 m³)`,
  olifant `(5000 kg)`, voetbalveld `(0,7 ha)`, film `(120 min)`.

Generator, four forms equally likely:

- reference / unit: a uniform item, `choiceStep` with the fixed options. Key
  `measureSense:reference:<index>`, `measureSense:unit:<index>`.
- instrument: a uniform item; distractors 3 of the instruments other than the answer and its
  `excluded`, via `shuffle`; options shuffled. Key `measureSense:instrument:<index>` (the
  distractors are not part of the key: the same item twice is a duplicate).
- ordering: `ORDER_UNITS = mm, cm, dm, m` (each once). Per unit a value
  `randomMantissa × 10^e`, `e ∈ [−1, 2]`, kept when it has at most 1 decimal, lies in
  `[0,1; 999]` and its size in `[5 mm, 20 m]`; redraw (`drawUntil`) until all sizes differ by at
  least 10% and the bare-number order differs from the size order. Direction 50/50.
  `orderingOptions(lengths, direction)` (exported, pure): the right order, the bare-number
  order, and two of the three neighbour swaps of the right order that differ from the bare
  order (`null` when fewer than two remain → redraw); options shuffled. Item text
  `${formatRational(value)} ${unit}`, joined with ` – ` (U+2013). Prompt
  `Welke rij staat van klein naar groot?` / `… van groot naar klein?`; explanation
  `in cm: 0,52 – 76 – 200 – 1040` in the right order. Key
  `measureSense:order:<direction>:<values in unit order>`.

### Task 9: `measureProblems` (§5.50)

Files: new `lib/topics/measureProblems.ts` + test; `types.ts`, `index.ts` (label
`Rekenen met maten (omrekenen in contexten)`).

- Units from `measurement.ts`: length `mm, cm, dm, m, km`, capacity `ml, cl, dl, L`, mass
  `mg, g, kg` (no `dam`, `hm`, cubic units or aliases in sums). `inUnit(value, from, to)` via
  `powerOfTen`.
- fit: `glazen` (`Hoeveel glazen van 25 cl kun je vullen uit een pak van 1,5 L?`, pak or fles),
  `stukken` (`Een plank van 2,4 m wordt in stukken van 30 cm gezaagd. Hoeveel stukken zijn
  dat?`), `porties` (`Een zak rijst van 2 kg wordt verdeeld in porties van 125 g. Hoeveel porties
  zijn dat?`); count whole in `[3, 40]`; suffix the noun; explanation `1,5 L = 150 cl → 150 :
  25 = 6`.
- mixed sum: dimension uniform, answer unit `u` uniform, three terms in units within 10³ of `u`,
  at least two distinct; term `randomMantissa × 10^e`, at most 2 decimals and at most 999; 30%
  one `−` at a random position; partial results positive; answer > 0, ≤ 10 000, ≤ 2 decimals
  (`drawUntil`). Prompt `1,2 m + 45 cm + 8 dm = ${SLOT} cm`, suffix `u`; explanation
  `1,2 m = 120 cm, 8 dm = 80 cm → 120 + 45 + 80 = 245` (terms already in `u` not converted).
- total: `flesjes`, `boodschappen`, `hardlopen` as in the spec. Explanations
