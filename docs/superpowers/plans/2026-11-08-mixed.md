# Plan: Gemengd (mixed categories, v4 step 1)

Spec: §3.1 (row Gemengd), §3.2 (set choice), §3.3 (header), §3.5 (Opnieuw), §4.1, §4.2
(Gemengd quota), §12.5. Inline execution, TDD, one commit per task; `npm test` + `npm run check`
before each commit. No new topics, so no tips and no lessons.

## Approach

Gemengd is a pseudo set, `MIXED_SET`, so it flows through the existing screens unchanged: the
play header and results show `set.name`, `SetRow` and `SetGlyph` show its row and glyph. It is
not in `PRACTICE_SETS` (so the Lesstof tab never lists it); `App` appends it to the Oefenen list
only. `App` routes a Gemengd start to `buildMixedSession` instead of `buildSession`, and
`SetupScreen` shows the set choice instead of the topic list. Fouten oefenen needs no change:
`generatorsFor(MIXED_SET, …)` returns `GENERATORS` (all tables), matching the Tafels pill.

Alternative rejected: a discriminated union `PracticeSet | MixedSelection` on every screen. It
touches `PlayScreen`, `ResultScreen` and every `Screen` variant for no behavioural gain.

## Task 2 — `buildMixedSession` (`lib/session.ts`)

Files: `src/lib/session.ts`, `src/lib/session.test.ts`, `src/lib/types.ts` (export
`TopicWeight`).

```ts
/** Gemengd (spec §4.2): equal counts per set, then each set's own topic weights. */
export function buildMixedSession(
  sets: readonly PracticeSet[], // the counting sets (countingSets)
  level: Level,
  size: number,
  rng: Rng,
  generators: Record<Topic, Generator> = GENERATORS,
): Question[];
```

- Refactor first (green before and after): extract from `buildSession` the private helpers
  `planTopics(topics: readonly TopicWeight[], total: number, rng: Rng): Topic[]` (steps 2–3) and
  `generatePlan(plan: readonly Topic[], rng, generators): Question[]` (de-dup + shuffle, steps
  4–5). Keep the RNG call order of `buildSession` identical.
- Quick: `allocateQuotas(sets as weight-1 keys, size)`; per set `choosesTables(set)` → `'tables'`
  × count, else `planTopics(set.topics, count)`.
- Deep: `quick = shareCount(QUICK_SHARE_PERCENT, size)`; allocate `size − quick` over the sets →
  `planTopics(set.deep ?? [], …)`; allocate `quick` over the sets independently →
  `planTopics(set.topics, …)`.
- Empty `sets` with `size > 0`: `allocateQuotas` throws `RangeError` (the UI never starts it).

Test helper: map each topic to its set and kind (`quick`, `deep`, `tables` → Tafels) from
`PRACTICE_SETS`; first a test that no topic is in two sets (the helper relies on it). Seeded RNG,
the distribution tests over ≥ 200 seeds.

| # | Sets | Level | n | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | all 11 | quick | 15 | 15 questions; per-set counts are four 2s and seven 1s; no deep topics; `tables` count = Tafels' count |
| 2 | all 11 | quick | 5 | 5 sets with 1, 6 with 0; the sets with 1 vary over seeds |
| 3 | Tafels, Meten | quick | 15 | counts {8, 7}; every non-table question is a Meten Opfrisser topic (no 15% tables in Meten's part) |
| 4 | Meten only | quick | 15 | no `tables` (no tables share), all Meten Opfrisser topics |
| 5 | Bewerkingen + the other 10 | quick | 15 | when Bewerkingen gets 1: `orderOfOperations` or `smartCalculation`, never `properties`, both seen over seeds |
| 6 | 10 deep sets | deep | 15 | no `tables`; 3 quick topics from 3 different sets; 12 deep: two sets 2, eight 1 |
| 7 | Meten, Verhoudingen | deep | 10 | per set: 1 quick topic, and each of its 4 deep topics once |
| 8 | Meten | deep | 5 | 0 quick (below 10), 5 deep over its 4 topics (2 + 1 + 1 + 1) |
| 9 | Meten | deep | 50 | 10 quick, 40 deep |
| 10 | all 11 | quick | 50 | 50 questions; per-set counts differ by at most 1 |
| 11 | `[]` | quick | 15 | throws `RangeError` |
| 12 | same seed twice | quick | 15 | identical sessions (key per question) |
| 13 | `buildSession` | — | — | existing tests unchanged and green (refactor) |

- `isMixed(set)`: section "Welke sets?" with **Alle** (`pool` ids) and **Geen** (`[]`), and a

  high, 15 px); reuse `.pill`, with a modifier for the wrapping layout. A disabled pressed pill
  is greyed (check the existing disabled-button token in `app.css`; no raw colours).
